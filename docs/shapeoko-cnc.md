<div class="safety-warning">
    <h4>⚠️ Safety Requirements</h4>
    • Safety glasses and hearing protection required<br>
    • Secure workpiece with clamps or screws<br>
    • Check spindle speed and feed rates<br>
    • Keep hands away from cutting area during operation<br>
    • Ensure dust collection is active
</div>

<div class="info-card">
    <h3>Software & Materials</h3>
    <p><strong>Software:</strong> Carbide Create (preferred), Fusion 360, VCarve Pro | <strong>Files:</strong> G-code, SVG, DXF | <strong>Specs:</strong> Shapeoko 4, 17"x17"x3" cutting area</p>
    <p><strong>Safe Materials:</strong> Wood, MDF, plywood, soft plastics, aluminum (with proper speeds/feeds)</p>
    <p><strong>End Mills:</strong> 1/4" and 1/8" carbide end mills, V-bits for engraving</p>
</div>

<div class="info-card">
    <h3>Procedures</h3>
    <h3>Startup: (1) Power on CNC and computer (2) Connect Carbide Motion (3) Home machine (4) Install correct end mill (5) Secure workpiece (6) Set work zero (7) Load G-code (8) Start cut</h3>
    <h3>Shutdown: (1) Raise spindle to safe height (2) Remove workpiece (3) Clean chips/dust (4) Store end mills safely (5) Power off systems (6) Log usage</h3>
</div>
