<div class="safety-warning">
    <h4>⚠️ Safety Requirements</h4>
    • Never leave printer unattended during first layer<br>
    • Ensure proper ventilation for ABS/PETG printing<br>
    • Let hot end cool before maintenance<br>
    • Keep workspace clear of flammable materials<br>
    • Check bed adhesion before long prints
</div>

<div class="info-card">
    <h3>Software & Materials</h3>
    <p><strong>Software:</strong> PrusaSlicer (preferred), Cura, OctoPrint | <strong>Files:</strong> STL, OBJ, 3MF | <strong>Specs:</strong> Prusa i3 MK3S+, 250x210x210mm</p>
    <p><strong>Safe Materials:</strong> PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, Wood-fill, Metal-fill</p>
    <p><strong>Settings:</strong> 0.4mm nozzle, 0.2mm layer height recommended</p>
</div>

<div class="info-card">
    <h3>Procedures</h3>
    <h3>Startup: (1) Power on printer (2) Preheat bed/nozzle (3) Load filament (4) Level bed if needed (5) Load G-code (6) Start print (7) Monitor first layer</h3>
    <h3>Shutdown: (1) Wait for print completion (2) Let cool down (3) Remove print (4) Unload filament if changing (5) Power off (6) Log usage</h3>
</div>
